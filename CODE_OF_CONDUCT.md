# Code of conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of all backgrounds and levels of experience, and focus
on what is best for the people who use the software. Harassment of any kind is not acceptable.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately (see CITATION.cff). Reports are handled
confidentially.
